Logo

The Maintain family mark, the word Maintain, and PAVEMENTS letter-spaced to the full width of the wordmark. PAVEMENTS is drawn geometry, in the same stroke and cap height as the family's sub-words, never set in a font. Charcoal on light, white on charcoal.

Mark and app icon

Clear space on every side = the cap height of “Maintain”. Minimum width 120px.

  • DoUse the drawn files in assets/logo/. Charcoal on paper and white, white on charcoal and imagery.
  • DoOn photography, sit the white lockup over a charcoal scrim of at least 50%.
  • Don'tPlace the charcoal mark on a dark ground. It reads 1.1:1 there; the inversion is the rule.
  • Don'tStretch, outline, shadow, gradient-fill or recolour it, or re-set PAVEMENTS in a typeface.
