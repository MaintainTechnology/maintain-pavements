Durable facts that design must not contradict. Visual decisions live in

## What this is

**Maintain Pavements** (descriptor: *Hardstand & Industrial Surfaces*) is the
Maintain group's pavements brand. Its product, **SuperBase**, is a bonding agent
dosed into the yard base a site already has, mixed through the full depth with
water, graded to level, then rolled and compacted into a bound hardstand:
built with a grader, water cart and roller, with no paving crew and no pour.

This repo holds the brand kit and design system (`design-system/`), the
SuperBase promo work (`MaintainPavements/` vault, `video-studio/` Remotion
build) and the source footage (`videos/`).

## Audience

- Site and yard operators, plant managers and asset owners weighing "keep
  making it good" against "pour a slab". Time-poor, engineering-literate,
  allergic to hype.
- Designers, editors and agents producing Maintain Pavements visuals: decks,
  capability statements, video supers, social and web.

## Brand commitments (pinned)

- **Charcoal `#26282B` is the one brand colour** (user-supplied as the ground
  of the white chevron mark). The system is monochrome: no accent colour.
- **The logo is the Maintain family mark with the drawn PAVEMENTS sub-word**,
  charcoal on light and white on charcoal. PAVEMENTS is geometry, never font
  text.
- **Figtree + Inter**, sharp corners, hairline rules, numbers as heroes.
- **Status severity colours are functional, not brand.**

## Constraints

- **Claims.** Say nothing stronger than the capability statement. Every figure
  carries its qualifier; the approved wording and the must-not-say list live in
  `MaintainPavements/01 Jon's Plan/Claims Register.md`.
- **No invented commercial facts:** no prices, customers, legal entities or ABNs.
  Contact details come only from Jon's document (jon@maintain.com.au,
  maintain.com.au).
- **Design system tooling:** static files, no build step. Python 3 + Pillow for
  the graphics pipeline; the contrast check is stdlib only.
