## Deep analysis

### Process: what this shows about SuperBase

This is Jon's **step 04, Roll and compact** ([[Step 4 - Roll and Compact]]). The [[SuperBase Capability Statement - Rev A]] captions its step-04 photo *"04 — Wet rolling to final compaction"*, heads the page *"Compaction builds the bond. Testing proves it."*, and says the stage is where *"the surface is built, not coated"*. The machine matches the method: a **smooth drum roller**.

**Surface state (visible):**
- **Moisture:** the pad is uniformly dark grey and matte, which reads as damp (inference). There's no pooled water, no spray, and no wet sheen on the near surface. The far pad near the open end looks lighter under grazing backlight, which could be slight sheen or just reflection at a low angle. So the clip **can't confirm "wet rolling"**: no water is seen going on or standing.
- **Texture:** a tight, closed, fine matrix with scattered small stones and specks. No ravelling, windrows, loose stone lines or potholes visible.
- **Drum / tyre marks:** faint linear marks converge towards the roller (drum-pass edges, 3.0–4.5 s), plus curved dark tyre tracks from the rear tyres beside the roller (4.5–7.5 s). No deep drum imprint or shoving at the drum edge is visible.
- **Fall / levels:** the pad looks planar. A thin, straight pale line lies on the surface from 5.0 s. It could be a string line for levels, a chalk mark or a scratch; it can't be identified at this resolution (inference). Crossfall can't be judged from a handheld phone.
- **Compaction evidence:** only the activity itself. There's no density gauge, no test point and no before/after.
- **Drum:** coated in dry-looking grey-brown fines. No drum water spray system is visible.

**What it proves:** a smooth-drum roller compacts a dark-grey pad under a canopy, operated with worklights on in low light. The pad looks level and closed at this stage.
**What it doesn't prove:** that SuperBase was dosed into this layer, that the rolling is wet, the compacted density, depth, bond or strength, or cure time. Grading isn't shown, so there's nothing here for [[Step 3 - Grade to Level]] beyond a pad that already looks trimmed. Jon's PDF does use a still of this same roller (5335) for step 03, *"Trimmed to level and crossfall"*.
