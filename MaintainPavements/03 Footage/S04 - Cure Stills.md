## S04A: trafficked bound surface (the PDF's "Finished bound surface")

![[S04A - image (2).png|420]]

### Summary
A phone photo taken from standing height, looking steeply down and forward across a light grey-beige bound yard surface. The surface looks closed and tight, with no obvious loose stone, potholes, ruts or cracks at this resolution. Its story is written in tyre marks. Several curved bands of faint tread imprint sweep from the lower left toward the upper right, the pattern a vehicle leaves when it turns. Two dark, feathered tyre-scrub marks sit in the foreground and midground, where a tyre has twisted or skidded on the surface (inference). At the far edge, grey steel rings are stacked on edge, beside a green-painted stand, a yellow drum, a red skip bin marked "METAL RECYCLERS" and part of a white vehicle. This is a working yard surface that is **in service**. It is the image Jon captioned *"Finished bound surface"* on page 5 (Applications).

### Region-by-region

**Background (top ~12% of the frame, y ≈ 0-140 px)**
- **Left third:** at least seven or eight large **grey steel rings** stand on edge, leaning in two main groups (about four at x ≈ 0-218 and three or four at x ≈ 193-445). Each has a heavy outer band and a perforated inner flange (a ring of small holes). There are blue marker scribbles on some faces, not legible. They could be large wheel rims or reel or drum ends, but that isn't confirmed. Rusty brown items sit at the extreme left edge.
- **Centre:** a green-painted steel stand or frame with red parts, a **yellow drum** and dark items behind it.
- **Centre-right:** a large **red skip bin** runs off the top edge. The visible lettering is white. The upper word is cut off by the frame, and the line under it reads "METAL RECYCLERS". This is a third-party metal-recycling bin. It is **not** a Maintain Pavements asset.
- **Top-right corner:** part of a white vehicle (cab or body) with yellow parts beneath. It can't be identified.
- **Ground at the far edge:** a darker brown band along the base of the rings and the bin, from tracked-in soil or damp (not certain), plus a pale grey patch in front of the bin.
- **Lighting:** flat and diffuse, with no cast shadows under the rings or the bin.

**Midground (y ≈ 140-620 px)**
- At least four or five **curved bands of tyre-tread imprint**, pale and dark in turn. They arc from lower-left to upper-right and curve away to the right, the geometry of vehicles turning across the area (inference). The imprints are **surface-level**. They look like tread texture pressed into a fine dust film (inference), with no ruts or displacement visible at this resolution.
- Tonal mottling between warm beige patches and cooler blue-grey patches. Mean colour here is about **#AAA496**, a warm light grey.

**Foreground (y ≈ 620-1195 px)**
- **Right scrub mark** (x ≈ 720-940, y ≈ 630-800): a dark grey crescent about 200 px long with a **chevron/feathered tread pattern** and possibly a slight sheen. It sits on top of the surface, with no visible gouge, rut or loosened stone around it.
- **Left scrub mark** (x ≈ 190-420, y ≈ 780-1080): a longer dark crescent with the same feathered chevron pattern, running from the lower-left up toward the centre. The same observation applies: the mark is on the surface and there's no displaced material next to it.
- These look like tyre scrub from a loaded wheel turning tightly or spinning (inference). The tread pattern is chevron-like, but no tyre or vehicle type can be identified from it.
- **Texture** (checked on 2.5x crops of both bottom corners): a dense, closed-looking matrix of fine particles speckled dark and light. There is no obvious loose aggregate and no ravelled patch at this resolution. Small particles are visible, but a photo can't show whether any of them are loose. Mean colour in the bottom quarter is about **#8C897D**.
- **Objects:** one small pale twig or bit of litter at about x 130, y 940. Nothing else.

**Moisture:** the surface reads **dry**. There's no sheen except on the scrub marks, and no ponding. The only possibly damp area is the darker brown band at the far edge.

**Edges:** no pad edge, kerb or boundary appears in the frame. The top edge cuts through the skip-bin lettering, which is itself evidence of a crop.

### What it proves about SuperBase
- **It's in service.** Vehicles have driven and turned on this surface. That supports the *return to service* half of [[Step 5 - Cure and Return to Service]].
- **It takes turning load without breaking up (visually).** Scrub and turning marks, with no visible ravelling, potholes, ruts or loose stone, support **C10** "holds its shape instead of ravelling into ruts and potholes" and the problem statement on PDF page 2 (loose stone, ravelling, rutting where plant turns). See [[Claims Register]]. That support is *visual only*. A photo can't show strength or age.
- **Bound and closed rather than loose.** It fits **C6** "built, not coated" only in the sense that nothing loose is visible. The photo can't show binder depth.
- **What it does NOT prove:** how soon after the final roll it was trafficked (**C2**, "within hours"), any CBR figure (**C9**), that this is a SuperBase site, or which yard it is. It also shows no organic top coat that can be identified.
