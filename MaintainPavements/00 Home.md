## Key findings at a glance

- **No speech in any clip.** Voice-activity detection found zero speech in all seven videos; the "Thank you for watching" transcripts are Whisper hallucinations on machinery noise. Every clip is a continuous machinery bed, so the promo needs **voiceover and music**, with the natural sound tucked underneath.
- **Mostly portrait, not all.** Five of seven clips are **portrait 9:16 (576x1024)**; **V02A and V02B are landscape 1024x576**. All are low-bitrate WhatsApp re-encodes (0.9–1.6 Mbps), so a 1080x1920 master needs about a 1.9x upscale.
- **V03C = V04.** Same clip (identical audio, same 552 frames); V03C is the better copy. That cuts **127.4 s of video to 108.9 s unique**, and it leaves the cure folder with **no cure video at all**: V04 shows rolling.
- **Two misfiles.** V02B (in dose-and-mix) and V04 (in cure) both show **Step 4 rolling**.
- **Coverage is lopsided.** **Step 4 is well covered** (four clips, 65.3 s). Steps 1 and 2 have one clip each and show only part of the step: no scarifying, no **mixing through depth**. **Step 3 has no footage** (no grader anywhere). **Step 5 has two stills and no video**, and no traffic on the finished pad.
- **One site** (inference, high confidence): all videos and S04B are under the same open-sided steel canopy, with red soil, bushland, red-flag pegs, IBC totes and the same roller 5335 in four clips. S04A is a different yard.
- **Timestamps are save times.** The WhatsApp order (dose 14 Aug, prepare 16 Aug, rolling 17 and 22 Aug) doesn't follow the process order, though the pad's physical state does. The light points to at least three filming sessions.
- **Four of Jon's five brochure step photos are frames from these clips**, but his step-03 photo is a roller (not a grader) and his step-05 photo is a different site. S04B would suit his step 05 better.
- **Strongest shots:** V02A 20.5 s (water cart hero), V02B 0.5–3.0 s (wet rolling with the hose), V03B 6.5 s (golden portrait roller), V03C 0.0–4.0 s, V01 2.5–3.0 s (Jon's Prepare frame), and **S04B as the end card**.
- **Handle with care:** the spray shots look like a *surface spray* (Jon says SuperBase isn't one), so always cut them into a mix or roll shot. Third-party marks (CAT, "Hastings Deering", "740", "5335", "METAL RECYCLERS"), identifiable crew, and people on foot close to moving plant all need a decision.
