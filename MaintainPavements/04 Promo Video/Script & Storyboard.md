# Script & Storyboard

Back to [[00 Home]] · [[Promo Video Brief]] · [[Shot Library]] · [[CapCut Edit Guide]] · [[Remotion Build Notes]] · [[AI Generation Prompts]] · [[Claims Register]]

Three cuts of the same story, in Jon's order: **Problem → Reframe → Method (prepare → dose and mix → grade to level → roll and compact → cure) → Proof → Ask.** Real footage carries every method beat. AI shots appear only in the problem and reframe beats and are labelled on screen. Diagrams are motion graphics, not footage.

## How to read the tables

| Notation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **V01 0.0–3.0** | Clip ID and exact source in-out in seconds, taken from the verified promo spans in the footage notes (frame filenames are on a 0.5 s grid). Never V04: it's a lower-bitrate duplicate of V03C |
| **full-bleed** | Portrait source (576x1024) scaled 1.875x to fill 1080x1920 |
| **1:1 window x N** | Landscape source (1024x576): a 576x576 crop starting at source x = N, scaled 1.875x to 1080x1080 and placed on charcoal `#25282A`, with the step counter and super in the panel below |
| **AI-GEN A1…B1** | Generated shot, prompt in [[AI Generation Prompts]]. Always carries the label `AI-generated · illustrative` |
| **GFX** | Motion graphic built in CapCut or Remotion (brand panel, diagram, end card) |
| **Kicker** | Short rule + wide-tracked caps label, e.g. `— HOW IT IS BUILT` |
| **Two-tone** | Headline with a grey `#99A1A7` setup line and a white payoff line (Jon's cover treatment) |
| **Step counter** | `01 PREPARE` … `05 CURE AND RETURN TO SERVICE`, with a five-segment progress bar |

Timing is at 30 fps (frames = seconds × 30). The music is 120 BPM, so one beat is 0.5 s and every cut below lands on a beat. The micro-disclaimer is the verbatim [[Claims Register]] text and stays on screen for at least 6 s in every cut.
