## Things we must NOT say or imply
- ✗ Any specific CBR uplift number or "x times stronger". The document deliberately avoids one.
- ✗ "Guaranteed", "permanent", "maintenance-free", "never pothole again".
- ✗ "Replaces concrete" without qualification (SuperBase is "not a substitute for engineered ground assessment").
- ✗ "Cheaper than concrete" as an absolute. Always say *indicatively*, vs *engineered concrete hardstand*.
- ✗ "Eco-friendly", "non-toxic", etc. The document makes no environmental claims, so don't invent them.
- ✗ Implying the footage site is a named customer unless Jon confirms.

## Suggested end-card micro-disclaimer (≤ 2 lines)
> *Figures indicative only. Rates, cure times and results vary with base material, climate and duty, and are confirmed by site trial and testing. Comparison vs engineered concrete hardstand.*
