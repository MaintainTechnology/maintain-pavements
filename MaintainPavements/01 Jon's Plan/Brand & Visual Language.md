# Brand & Visual Language

Everything here was taken from the PDF itself: colours were read from the vector fills and text spans in the file, and layout and type were observed from the rendered pages.

## Logo
- A **mark of two slanted, stacked parallelogram strokes** forming a stylised "M" (road-lane / chevron feel), shown white on charcoal.
- Wordmark: **"Maintain"** (bold, title case) over **"PAVEMENTS"** (small, wide-tracked caps).
- Descriptor: **HARDSTAND & INDUSTRIAL SURFACES** (tiny, wide-tracked caps, grey).
- In the page headers, a small dark mark plus **MAINTAIN PAVEMENTS · SUPERBASE FOR HARDSTAND**.

> [!warning] We don't have a vector logo file in the repo. Ask Jon for an SVG or PNG logo before the edit.

**Character:** a near-monochrome, *industrial-premium* scheme with **no accent colour**. The only colour on the pages comes from the site photos (hi-vis orange, CAT yellow, red survey pegs, blue sky, red-dirt Australia).
**For video:** keep the graphics charcoal/white/grey and let the footage supply the colour. If an accent is needed, borrow **hi-vis orange** or **CAT yellow** from the footage, and use it sparingly.

## Typography
- **Headlines:** a heavy, geometric grotesk with tight tracking (visually close to *Figtree / Satoshi / General Sans*). Two-tone treatment on the cover: grey setup, then a **white payoff**.
- **Body:** a neutral humanist sans (visually close to *Inter*).
- **Labels:** small uppercase with **very wide letter-spacing** (e.g. `T H E  M A I N T E N A N C E  C Y C L E`), usually after a short horizontal rule.
- **Numbers as heroes:** "60–70%" set huge and bold on a charcoal panel, with a tracked-caps caption underneath.
- The PDF embeds **Type3 (rasterised) fonts**, so the real font names can't be recovered from it. **Ask Jon for the font files or names.** Stand-ins: **Figtree** (headlines) and **Inter** (body), both on Google Fonts, which suits Remotion.

## Layout motifs to reuse in motion graphics
1. **Section kicker:** a short bar plus tracked caps (e.g. `— HOW IT IS BUILT`).
2. **Numbered steps:** big `01`–`05` numerals beside the step name, which works well as a step-counter overlay on the footage.
3. **Two-tone headline:** grey setup line, then a white or ink payoff line.
4. **Big-number panel:** charcoal box, huge figure, tiny tracked caption.
5. **Photo captions** in tracked caps: `04 — WET ROLLING TO FINAL COMPACTION`. These can be reused directly as lower-thirds.
6. Generous margins, thin hairline rules, and no rounded corners, gradients or icons.

## Photography style (from the PDF and footage)
- Real, unstaged site photos from phones: plant working, low-angle drum shots, and wide finished-yard shots under hard Australian light.
- The strongest images are the **cover hero** (a finished yard in front of a shed) and the **low wet-rolling drum** shots.
