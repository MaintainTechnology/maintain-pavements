# Process Overview

How SuperBase is built, step by step, as Jon describes it in [[SuperBase Capability Statement - Rev A]], and what our footage really shows for each step. The footage notes in `03 Footage` are the evidence; this note joins them up. Everything marked **(inference)** is a reading, not something visible.

Map: [[00 Home]] · [[Footage Index]] · visual flow: [Process Flow.canvas](Process%20Flow.canvas) · claims: [[Claims Register]]

## 1. The method in Jon's words

> SuperBase is a bonding agent dosed into the yard base you already have. It is mixed through with water, graded to level, then rolled and compacted into a hard, bound hardstand — built with a grader, water cart and roller, with no paving crew and no pour. (Cover, p.1)

> SuperBase is not a pavement, and its primary mode is not a surface spray. It is an additive introduced into the base during the lay phase and carried through every moisture process, so the binder sits through the full depth of the layer rather than on top of it. (p.3)

The principle behind the five steps: **the binder goes *into* the existing base, not *onto* it**, and the surface gets its strength from compaction, not from a coating. Water is the enemy throughout: it's what breaks an unbound yard down (p.2), it's what the crossfall has to shed (p.3), and it's the condition the CBR test is run in (p.4, "soaked").

## 8. The proof loop (CBR)

Jon's method doesn't stop at the roller. The engagement wraps the five steps in a test-before, test-after loop (p.4 and p.6):

1. **Yard walk.** Review the areas, the base material and what the surface costs to hold each year.
2. **Trial area.** Nominate one representative area.
3. **CBR before.** Laboratory **soaked** CBR on your own yard material, before treatment.
4. **Treat.** Steps 01 to 05 above, with the dosage designed for that material.
5. **Density on site.** Compaction confirmed against the density nominated in the dosage design (step 04).
6. **CBR after cure.** Same soaked test, same material.
7. **Trial report.** Soaked CBR before and after, treated depth and density achieved, and the dosage rate that produced it.
8. **Roll out by area**, staged so the yard keeps trading, then **hand over** to the customer's own crew and plant.

**None of the loop is in the footage**: no density gauge, no sampling, no lab. It's the "measured, not asserted" half of Jon's pitch (C9), so it needs its own shots or a motion-graphic beat. Never put a CBR number on screen; the document deliberately gives none.
