## Why it matters

Jon's own causal chain is the clearest piece of engineering logic in the document:

**no fall → water sits on the surface → it soaks into the base under traffic → the base softens and the stones work loose → a pothole starts** ("ponded water is where potholes start").

- **Fall (crossfall)** is the slight designed slope that sheds water off the surface. Get it right and rain runs off; get it wrong and every low spot becomes a puddle, then a pothole (C10).
- **"While it is still workable."** The base is trimmed *after* the binder goes in and *before* it's rolled. Once the treated layer has been compacted and has bonded, correcting the levels would mean cutting into a bound layer (inference), so the fall has to be set in this window.
- **Level for plant.** Plant standing on out-of-level ground is one of Jon's p.2 problems. A level standing area is the p.5 outcome.
- **The grader is the tool.** It's what makes C5 "grader, water cart and roller" true, and it's the machine Jon promises "stops coming back" (C11) once the surface is bound.
